Privacy Policy: webaco

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · webaco home page

This policy explains how the webaco application handles data. webaco is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, Vitor Coimbra, to upload and schedule videos on his own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services.

1. Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants consent, webaco requests these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload video files to the authorized channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl: to set or update the metadata of the videos the owner uploads (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, privacy status and scheduled publish time).

Google gives the app an OAuth access token and a refresh token for the authorized channel.

The app does not access:

  • the YouTube data of other users;
  • comments, subscribers, messages, contacts, email or payment information.

2. How the data is used

The tokens are used only to perform the actions the owner requests: uploading the owner's videos and setting their metadata and schedule. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

  • The tokens are stored only as local files on the owner's personal computer.
  • They are never sent to any server operated by the app; the app has no server.
  • The only network requests go directly to Google and YouTube APIs over HTTPS.

4. Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

  • The tokens are kept only while the owner uses the app.
  • The owner can delete them at any time by deleting the local token files.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, the tokens stop working.

6. Limited Use

webaco's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. YouTube and Google terms

webaco uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to:

  • the YouTube Terms of Service;
  • the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used by a single adult owner.

9. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: webacoio@gmail.com.

webaco

webaco is a personal desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels.

What the app does

  • Uploads video files that the owner produced to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and publish date (scheduling) of those videos.

It uses the official YouTube Data API v3, with the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in.

Who uses it

It is used only by its owner, Vitor Coimbra, on his own computer, to manage his own channels. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it accesses no other user's data.
